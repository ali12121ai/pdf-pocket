# Privacy Policy (draft — publish before store release)

PDF Pocket ("we", "the app") processes documents on your device. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## What we do NOT do

- No account or sign-in is required.
- Files, PDFs, scanned pages and OCR text are processed locally and are not uploaded to our servers. We have no document servers.
- We do not sell personal data.

## What the app stores on your device

- Recent file records in a local Room database.
- A local preference recording whether ads were removed.
- Temporary working files in the app cache, cleaned after 24 hours.
- Arabic OCR model data (bundled in the app and copied to app-private storage on first use).

Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

## What third-party SDKs process

- **Google AdMob** may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data to serve ads. Ads are shown only after required consent in regions where consent is required.
- **Google Play Billing** processes the optional one-time "remove ads" purchase. We receive only the purchase state, not your payment details.
- **Google User Messaging Platform** stores your consent choice on the device.

## Permissions

- Camera: only when you choose to scan a document.
- Internet: needed only to load ads and process purchases.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
